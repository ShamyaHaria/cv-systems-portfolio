## Reflection

For this assessment, I was assigned the topic of non-linear activation functions in artificial neural networks and convolutional neural networks. I covered five key activation functions — Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU, Leaky ReLU, and Softmax — explaining their formulas, properties, strengths, and weaknesses, as well as where each one is used in a CNN pipeline.

The most challenging part was explaining the vanishing gradient problem clearly in a short amount of time. I found it helpful to use the graph of the Sigmoid curve to visually show how it flattens at the extremes, which makes it easier to understand why gradients die out. I also focused on building the explanation progressively — starting with why non-linearity is needed at all, before introducing each function, so the audience has context throughout.

Overall, I feel this topic sits naturally in the course after the introduction to ANN nodes and basic network structure, and leads directly into understanding convolution layers and CNN architecture.
